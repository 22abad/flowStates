# The €763 Problem: A Day of Rage, Focus, and Asset Forging

Today began with a conflict and ended with a revelation. The conflict was with a two-meter-tall security guard. The revelation was the tangible, calculated value of a single LeetCode problem. This is the story of how anger was converted into assets.

## Act I: The Conflict That Fueled the Engine

This morning, I had a 20-minute debate with a security guard named Anton. He insisted only PhDs and staff were on his list to enter the building during the holidays. My old self might have walked away. But today, I switched back into lawyer mode.

I calmly presented my case. First, I cited the official email from James Cotter, which explicitly stated the building was open to postgraduates, listing only four specific closure dates, which I had respected. Second, I established precedent: my classmates and I had been studying here for the past two days without issue. When he mentioned his "list," I requested his superior's contact information to verify the policy. After a phone call where he kept repeating "PhD," I held my ground on the email's wording: "It says postgraduate, and I am a postgraduate."

He finally relented. The time I won back by asserting my rights felt infinitely more valuable than time freely given. It created a sense of urgency, a rage against wasted potential. That conflict became the catalyst for my most productive day in Ireland.

## Act II: The Harvest of Hyper-Focus

Fueled by that morning's "anger," I achieved a state of hyper-focus. The result was a personal record: **12 LeetCode problems, all accepted, in a single day.**

- **Morning Session: Greedy Algorithms.** I swept through a series of Greedy problems. The "choose the best option now" nature of Greedy was the perfect channel for that aggressive energy.
- **Afternoon Session: Dynamic Programming.** I tackled the harder subject of DP, progressing from foundational problems like Climbing Stairs to 2D pathfinding problems optimized to a 1D array.

The contrast to my early days is stark. I recall spending six hours on a simple logic game, unable to translate the rules into code. Today, while I still needed a structural hint for a complex DP problem with obstacles (LC 63), I could debug with fluency. The confidence born from this tangible, logic-driven progress is the most effective form of motivation.

## Act III: The True Value of "Accepted"

This surge in productivity led to a critical reframing. I stopped seeing LeetCode as "studying"—a tedious, anti-human task. I started seeing it as **"mining."** Every green "Accepted" isn't just a checkmark; it's a tangible asset being forged.

This isn't about forecasting a future salary, but about calculating present opportunity cost. I am investing the same intensity of focus required to win a complex legal case into mastering these algorithms. Every line of code represents a direct conversion of that high-value professional time. Knowledge is being reforged into a new, higher-dimension class of capital, and I am in the midst of an aggressive asset restructuring.

But at 43, this calculation transcends money. This is likely my last, best chance to execute such a profound cognitive retooling. The opportunity to perfect the mind, to race along this path of logical mastery, is an asset whose value cannot be quantified.

## Act IV: The Path Forward

This new framework—seeing each problem as a high-value asset—makes motivation a non-issue. The drive to solve more, to achieve more, becomes immense.

Yes, this intense focus has a side effect: a growing sense of isolation. Conversations with peers about daily trifles feel increasingly distant. Is the air simply thinner on the way up? Perhaps. But I know I will continue to go to Eolas, not just for the dual 4K monitors, but for the possibility of human connection. Code can be written in solitude, but a person cannot be an island.

Good night, Anton. Good night, Dublin. Tomorrow, the mining continues.

# 763 欧元的算法题：愤怒、专注与资产铸造的一天

坐标：Dublin, Ireland 天气：心中有火，手里有码

今天的故事，从一场冲突开始，以一个顿悟结束。冲突的对象，是一个两米高的保安。而顿悟的内容，是一道 LeetCode 题究竟值多少钱。这是一个将愤怒转化为资产的故事。

## 第一幕：点燃引擎的冲突

早上在 Eolas 门口碰到了保安 Anton，这哥们儿也是绝了，俄罗斯人，快两米的大高个，壮得像堵墙，却非说只有 PhD 和 staff 才能进。

如果是以前，我可能就耸耸肩走了。但今天不行。我直接切换回了律师模式，跟他掰扯了 20 分钟。

首先，我拿出学校 James Cotter 的群发邮件，指出邮件明确写了整个假期只有 25、26、31 和 1 号四天不建议来，我遵守了规则。其次，我告诉他，昨天和前天，我和另外三个同学都在这里学习到下午 6 点，有先例可循。他还是嘴硬，说他的名单上没有我。我说：“那你有领导电话吗？我给她打电话或发邮件。”他说人家在放假。我又补充道：“我们 CS 的 Hope 老师前两天还发邮件关心我们国际生的情况，学校是支持我们来学习的。”

他给领导打了个电话，嘴里还在念叨“PhD”，我直接打断他：“邮件写的是 Postgraduate，我就是 Post！”。经过他与电话那头的再次确认，最后还是让我进了。

我感谢他，想递根烟缓和一下，结果掏出来的是电子烟，shit，搞了个乌龙。我俩都笑了，约好下次一起抽。他开车走了，应该没有不开心。

争来的权利才最珍贵。坐在工位上，感受着久违的暖气，我觉得今天的每一秒都是从牙缝里抢回来的，必须倍加珍惜。

## 第二幕：超高效率的收获

带着这股“怒气”，我进入了超聚焦状态。结果是创下个人记录：**一天之内，12 道 LeetCode，全部 AC。**

- **上午：** 贪心算法横扫千军。“只看当下”的特性，完美地疏导了那股攻击性的能量。
- **下午：** 啃下了动态规划这块硬骨头，从“爬楼梯”一路打到“二维路径压缩”。

回想刚开始时，一道简单的逻辑题我能憋 6 个小时也无法落实到代码。而今天，虽然遇到 LC 63 这种难题仍需提示，但我已能流畅地 Debug。这种逻辑闭环带来的自信，比任何鸡汤都管用。

## 第三幕：“Accepted”的真实价值

这次效率的爆发，带来一个关键的思维转变。我不再把刷题看作反人性的“学习”，而是看作**“挖矿”**。每一个绿色的 "Accepted"，都是在铸造一份实实在在的资产。

这笔账，并非预支未来，而是衡量当下的机会成本。我将过去在法庭上赢得一个复杂案件所需投入的同等专注与心力，悉数注入到这些算法之中。每一行代码的背后，都是真金白银的时间价值在进行置换。知识正在被重塑为新的、更高维度的资本，而我，正在全速进行资产重组。

但对于 43 岁的我而言，这笔账远超金钱。这很可能是我人生最后一次，也是最好的一次，进行如此底层和深刻的“心智重构”的机会。抓住它，让心智臻于完美，这个机会本身的价值，无可估量。

## 第四幕：前路

有了这个“挖矿”的新框架，动力问题迎刃而解。去解决更多问题、铸造更多资产的欲望变得无比强烈。

是的，高强度的专注带来了孤独感。当周围人还在谈论琐事时，我的世界已经不同。或许，人在上坡路上，注定要忍受稀薄的空气。但我知道，我仍会去 Eolas，不只为那里的双 4K 显示器，也为保留一丝与世界连接的可能性。代码可以独处，但人不能是一座孤岛。

晚安，Anton。晚安，都 блин。明天，继续挖矿。

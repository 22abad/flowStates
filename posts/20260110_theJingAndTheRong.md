# To My Clumsy, Passionate Self: Finding My *Jianghu* on a Grey Irish Morning

**Time:** 7:00 AM
**Location:** Maynooth, Ireland
**BGM:** "Iron Blood, Loyal Heart" / "The Great Hero"

The Irish sky is its usual shade of grey.

Walking to the university, my fingers, as if guided by some ghost from the past, scrolled to a song from my childhood. When the grand, symphonic prelude exploded through my headphones, when those familiar battle cries—"Hoo! Ha! Hoo! Ha!"—reverberated across my cerebral cortex...

I, a 43-year-old man on a street in a foreign country, suddenly burst into tears.

These were not tears of sorrow. Not at all. They were tears of overwhelming joy, the tremor of a soul struck by a heavy blow, the eruption of a volcano long suppressed.

In this soaring melody, I saw my "mediocre" self, walking step by step on the path to becoming a *Xia*—a hero of my own story.

## 1. At 43, I Will Be My Own "Guo Jing"

Looking at the song title, "The Great Hero," I was struck by a thought: I never imagined that at 43, I would have truly left everything behind to chase a dream.

In Jin Yong's novels, Guo Jing is dull, slow, and learns everything slower than others. But it is this same Guo Jing who, through his stubborn, almost foolish persistence, cultivates his inner strength day after day, eventually achieving an exponential growth in his martial arts to become a great hero of his generation.

Isn't that me?

From the day I arrived in Ireland, in Maynooth, I have been the "underachiever" always trying to catch up. In age, I am an uncle. In background, I am a liberal arts major switching to code. In foundation, I started from nearly zero. In language and life adaptation, I struggled. Against the backdrop of younger faces, my aptitude could be described as "poor."

But I told myself: I may not be the smartest, but I must, absolutely must, be the most hardworking.

## 2. The Days Fueled by Monster and Grit

The path to heroism has no shortcuts, only arduous journeys.

These tears, perhaps, are for the self who gritted his teeth and persevered. Looking back on this path of self-reinvention, it was so bitter, and so incredibly燃 (burning with passion).

**I don't fear being slow; I only fear stopping.** Faced with the cryptic complexities of Java, I could spend two weeks wrestling with 2-3 hours of course content until every piece of logic was seared into my brain.

**I don't fear the distance; I only fear the delay.** Living in a shared flat in Dublin for €400 a month, I woke up at 5:30 AM every day to catch the early class in Maynooth, returning home at 12:30 AM. I endured this for three solid months.

**I am not reckless with my life, only with my time.** For one week, to force myself into a "life acceleration mode," I chugged 5-6 cans of Monster energy drinks daily. I couldn't bear to waste time queuing for coffee, let alone sleeping. I still remember the feeling of my heart pounding, nearly fainting on the way to the train station.

**I was possessed, and I was in a state of deep focus.** My mind had room for nothing but LeetCode problems. Several times, I became so engrossed in solving problems on the train that I missed my stop, only to look up and find myself in Mullingar (in central Ireland), feeling a strange sense of fulfillment on an unfamiliar platform.

I never complained once. Because I knew that when a person truly wants to reach for the heights, the entire world will make way for them, even if that path is paved with Monster cans and sleep deprivation.

## 3. My "Huang Rong" is My Greatest Strength

I have no earth-shattering achievements, but I have the greatest luck in the world.

In this hero's symphony, I thought of my wife back in China. If I am the clumsy Guo Jing, learning to fly by starting early, she is the brilliant, strategic Huang Rong who has always been my unwavering support.

She is in China by herself, taking care of our child who will be two this month, looking after both sets of our parents, and charging ahead in her own career. My wife is 12 years younger than me, in the prime of her life and at a rising stage in her career. Though we are on opposite sides of the world, we are both fighting hard in our respective battles. Yesterday, she told me she was just promoted to Legal Director of her corporate group.

Reading her message, I was overcome with emotion. She is resilient, intelligent, hardworking, with a grand vision and an even grander heart. It was she who told me, "You are top-tier!" And it is she who, with her slender yet tenacious shoulders, has carried our family, giving this 43-year-old "old boy" the freedom to chase his dreams in a foreign land.

I am so, so lucky.

## 4. Embrace This Rebirth

Right now, it is 7 AM in Maynooth. The Irish sky is still overcast, the wind still cold, but my state of mind is completely different.

The song "Iron Blood, Loyal Heart" is still echoing in my headphones. *"Having been through a hundred calamities, the bond of love and righteousness is unbreakable."*

I am no longer the anxious middle-aged man, no longer the liberal arts student intimidated by code. I wipe the tears from my face. I know this is a baptism, a transformation.

I have completely embraced this new self.

I will continue down this path, just like Guo Jing—clumsily, steadfastly, one step at a time. I must be worthy of my "Huang Rong," and worthy of the man who almost fainted on the road for the sake of his ideals.

*Hoo! Ha!* Hello, new me. Good morning, Maynooth.

***Author's Note:*** *This piece is dedicated to all the middle-aged people who are still fighting on their path at this very moment. As long as the heart's fire is not extinguished, the spirit of the hero remains, and so does youth. Hand in hand with our loved ones, we march towards our happiness. See you in the Jianghu.*

# 致笨拙而热烈的我们：在灰暗的爱尔兰清晨，我看见了心中的江湖

**时间：** 清晨 7:00
**地点：** 爱尔兰，美努斯（Maynooth）
**BGM：** 《铁血丹心》/《侠之大者》

爱尔兰的天空，像往常一样灰蒙蒙的。

走在通往学校的路上，不知道为什么，手指突然鬼使神差地划到了幼时听过的老歌。当交响乐团那宏大的前奏铺天盖地而来，当那一声声熟悉的“呼！哈！呼！哈！”在耳机里炸裂，瞬间回荡在整个大脑皮层时——

我，一个43岁的中年男人，在异国他乡的街头，突然泪流满面。

这不是悲伤的眼泪，绝不是。这是喜极而泣，是灵魂被重击后的震颤，是压抑许久的火山终于找到了出口。

在这段激昂的旋律里，我看见了那个“资质平庸”的自己，正一步步走在成为“侠”的路上。

## 一、 43岁，我要做自己的“郭靖”

看着歌单里《侠之大者》的标题，我突然对自己感到惊讶：我从没想过，43岁的我，竟然真的抛下了一切，还在追求理想的路上狂奔。

金庸笔下的郭靖，木讷、迟钝，学什么都比别人慢。但也是这个郭靖，靠着那股子近乎执拗的傻劲，日复一日地修炼内功，最终迎来了武功的指数级增长，终成一代大侠。

这不就是我吗？

从来爱尔兰，来美努斯的第一天起，我就是那个永远在追赶的“后进生”。 论年龄，我是大叔；论背景，我是文科生转码；论基础，我几乎是从零开始；论语言和生活适应，我步履维艰。在周围年轻面孔的映衬下，我的资质可以说是“很差”的。

但我告诉自己：我可以不是最聪明的，但我一定，必须，要是那个最努力的！

## 二、 那些喝着Monster拼命的日子

成侠之路，从来就没有捷径，只有苦行。

现在的眼泪，大概是替过去那个咬牙坚持的自己流的。回望这段重塑自我的路，真的太苦，也太燃了。

**我不怕笨，只怕停。** 面对晦涩难懂的Java，为了2-3小时的课程内容，我可以死磕两周，直到把每一个逻辑都刻进脑子里。

**我不怕远，只怕晚。** 住在都柏林400欧一个月的群租房里，为了赶上去美努斯的早课，我每天清晨5:30起床，披星戴月；晚上回到住处已是半夜12:30。这样的日子，我硬是坚持了三个月。

**我不惜命，只惜时。** 有那么一个礼拜，为了强行开启“生命加速模式”，我每天灌下5-6瓶Monster能量饮料。我不舍得排队买咖啡，更舍不得把时间浪费在睡觉上。那种心脏狂跳、差点晕倒在去火车站路上的感觉，我现在还记得。

**我入了魔，也入了定。** 脑子里除了LeetCode的算法题，装不下任何东西。好几次在火车上刷题刷到忘我，一抬头发现车已经开到了Mullingar（爱尔兰中部），坐过了站，却在陌生的站台感到一种荒谬的充实。

我没有抱怨过一句。因为我知道，当一个人真正想去往高处时，全世界都会为他让路，哪怕那条路是用Monster和睡眠不足铺成的。

## 三、 顶配的“黄蓉”，是我最大的底气

我没有什么惊天动地的成就，但我有着这世上最顶级的运气。

在这首英雄交响曲中，我想到了远在国内的妻子。如果我是那个笨鸟先飞的郭靖，她就是那个聪明绝顶、运筹帷幄，始终在背后支撑我的黄蓉。

她一个人在国内，照顾着我们要在这个月才满两岁的宝宝，照顾着双方老人，还要在职场上冲锋陷阵。我妻子比我小12岁，正值花样年华，也处于事业的快速上升期，我们虽然身处世界两端，但同样在各自的战场上奋力拼搏。昨天她告诉我，她刚刚被提升为集团的法务总监。

看着她发来的消息，我感慨万千。她顽强、聪明、努力，有大格局，更有大胸怀。是她对我说：“你是顶配！” 也是她，用她柔弱却坚韧的肩膀，扛起了我们的小家，给了我这个43岁的“老男孩”在异国他乡逐梦的自由。

我太幸运了，真的。

## 四、 纵情一次，拥抱新生

此时此刻，美努斯清晨7点。爱尔兰的天空依旧阴沉，风依旧冷冽，但我的心境已彻底不同。

耳机里的《铁血丹心》还在回响，“身经百劫也在心间，恩义两难断”。

我不再是那个焦虑的中年人，不再是那个畏惧代码的文科生。我擦干脸上的泪水，我知道，这是蜕变后的洗礼。

我已彻底拥抱这个全新的自我。

这条路，我会继续走下去，像郭靖一样，笨拙地、坚定地、一步一个脚印地走下去。我要配得上我的“黄蓉”，配得上那个为了理想差点晕倒在路上的自己。

呼！哈！ 你好，全新的我。 早安，美努斯。
